# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive sales dashboard built using Power BI to analyze the performance of a retail superstore. The goal is to uncover key sales drivers, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
The business lacks clear visibility into sales performance across regions, categories, and customer segments. This makes it difficult to identify growth opportunities and optimize sales strategies.

This dashboard aims to:
- Analyze sales trends over time  
- Compare performance across regions and categories  
- Understand customer segment contribution  
- Evaluate the impact of discounts and shipping modes on sales  

---

## 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated  
- **Total Orders:** Number of unique orders  
- **Total Customers:** Unique customers  
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Revenue per order  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis
- Visualizes sales over time (2015–2018)  
- Helps identify growth patterns and trends  

### 2. Regional Performance
- Compares sales across regions (West, East, Central, South)  
- Highlights top-performing and underperforming areas  

### 3. Category Analysis
- Breaks down sales by product category  
- Identifies which categories drive revenue  

### 4. Customer Segment Insights
- Shows contribution from Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments  
- Helps understand target customer groups  

### 5. Interactive Filters
- Category  
- Region  
- Ship Mode  
- Year range  

These allow users to dynamically explore the data.

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales  
- The **West region** generates the highest revenue  
- Sales show a **steady upward trend**, especially after 2016  
- A small number of categories drive the majority of revenue  

---

## ⚠️ Limitations
- The dataset does not include cost data, therefore **profitability analysis is not possible**  
- Insights are limited to **sales performance only**, not margins or efficiency
